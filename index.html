How to play

Read the rules

Each tile is an arrow pointing up, right, down, or left. Tap it to send it toward the nearest board edge. Its path must be empty. Remove the arrows in the right order until the board is clear.

Keyboard: focus the board, use ↑ ↓ ← → to select an arrow, then press Enter or Space. Every puzzle is generated with a guaranteed solution.
